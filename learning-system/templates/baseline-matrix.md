For project rows, distinguish:
- **Independent execution:** what the learner designed/wrote/tested without AI-generated solution steps.
- **AI-assisted execution:** what AI generated or materially guided, and what the learner reviewed or changed.
- **Explanation:** whether the learner can explain purpose, flow, dependencies, trade-offs, and limitations without reading generated explanations.
- **Modification/debugging:** whether the learner can make a bounded change and diagnose a new failure independently.

## Interpretation Rules

- **NOT ASSESSED:** no reliable evidence yet.
- **FAMILIAR:** recognizes terms and purpose.
- **EXPLAINED:** explains in own words.
- **APPLIED WITH HELP:** completes with hints, guidance, or AI assistance; record the exact assistance.
- **APPLIED INDEPENDENTLY:** solves a new representative task without solution steps and validates it.
- **EVALUATED:** detects errors and compares approaches with reasons.
- **DEVELOPED:** builds a reusable solution with tests/docs/validation.
- **NEEDS REVIEW:** previously learned but currently unreliable.
- **DEFERRED:** not needed in the current period, with a reason.

AI-assisted work is valid evidence of exposure and supported performance, but it is not evidence of independent execution unless the learner separately demonstrates the capability without solution guidance. Explanation and modification are assessed separately from authorship.
